Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers

Can UI/UX design come before development?

Yes. Exploring the journeys and prototype first can help clarify scope and surface usability questions early.

Is UI/UX only about visual appearance?

No. It includes information structure, usability, task flow, feedback and how screens behave when data or context changes.

Can you improve an existing product interface?

We can review its core journeys, identify friction and discuss a focused redesign rather than replacing everything at once.
