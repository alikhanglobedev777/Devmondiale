Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers

Do we need to replace our current software?

Not necessarily. We start by examining your current systems and whether supported APIs or integrations can connect them safely.

Can AI automate every customer interaction?

No. An assistant is best for defined tasks, while sensitive, unusual or high-stakes requests should have a clear human handoff.

How do we decide what to automate first?

A good starting point is a repetitive task with clear inputs, an understandable success condition and limited operational risk.
